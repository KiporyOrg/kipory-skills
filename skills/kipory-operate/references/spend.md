# Spend — what a step and each kind of run is charged

What a project is charged for: per step, and per kind of run. Where each amount is read, and why the reads disagree, is `references/spend-reads.md`; the balance, the 402 codes and the ceilings are `references/balance.md`. Every amount is **credits, and one credit is one micro-USD**: `x-credits-charged: 5139` is about half a US cent.

## What a step is charged

A step that runs a function is charged for its compute, and for whatever it called or wrote. `GET /v1/runs/{runId}/spend` lists the charges per step under `byStep[].charges` — one entry per `kind`, and per `direction` on a model call — and they sum to the step's `credits`.

| `kind`                             | What it pays for                            | `units`                                                      |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `function-run`                     | the step's compute time                     | milliseconds billed                                          |
| `llm-call`                         | a model call, one entry per `direction`     | tokens (`in`, `out`, `cached`, `cache-write`)                |
| `embedding`                        | an embedding call                           | tokens                                                       |
| `transcription` · `rerank`         | audio transcription · a rerank call         | the unit the charge is priced in                             |
| `vendor-fetch`                     | a paid fetch the step made through a vendor | the unit the charge is priced in                             |
| `storage-upload` · `vector-upsert` | a file or a vector written                  | the unit the charge is priced in                             |
| `storage-delete`                   | a file removed                              | the unit the charge is priced in                             |
| `storage-held` · `vector-held`     | the daily charge for files and vectors kept | not a run's charge — read it on `usage` with `scope=holding` |

So a step that cost 46 reads as 10 for one second of compute (`units: 1000`) and 36 for the model.

### Compute

- **For the time the step ran, to the millisecond, with no minimum** — except a step that failed on a platform fault, which is charged nothing (below). At 10 credits a second a step that took 1.2 s costs 12, and one that took 40 ms costs nothing: each charge is rounded down to a whole credit.
- **The rate is the deployment's and no route lists it.** <!-- absent: GET /v1/nodes/{nodeId}/compute-rate --> Read it off a `function-run` charge of a second or more: `credits ÷ units` is credits per millisecond, so `× 1000` is per second.
- **A run with no model call is not free.** Each step that runs bills the time it took; only the very shortest come to under one credit.
- **Waiting is not billed.** A step that runs as a queued job — a model step, a fetch, `term.upsert` and the other ingest-phase steps — is charged for the time its job ran, summed over its tries. The model or vendor call itself is charged once across the function's own tries, and once per try when the step sets `tries`. The wait for a free worker, for a rate-limit allowance and between tries is not charged. A busy worker makes such a step slower, not dearer, and its `durationMs` on `GET /v1/runs/{runId}/timeline` can be many seconds longer than the time it was charged.
- **A job no worker took is not charged.** A queued step that is given up on because no worker picked its job up in time (class `platform-busy`) makes no compute charge, and the platform starts the run again (below).
- **A step that runs other steps inside itself is charged for its own work only.** A `vocabulary.resolve` step that runs a vocabulary's resolver flow is not charged for the time spent in that flow: the resolver flow's steps are each charged as their own. They are entries of their own in `byStep`, under the resolver flow's `flowId` and step keys — keys your flow never wrote.
- **A block makes no compute charge of its own.** `if`, `each`, `repeat`, `parallel`, `try`, `return`, `fail` and a `flow.invoke` call are not billed for compute; the steps they run are charged as usual.

### Models and vendors

- **Model prices are a read.** `GET /v1/nodes/{nodeId}/model-prices` (the project's id is a node id) lists every enabled model once per `operation` it bills, with `credits` for one `unit` (`million-tokens`, `minute` or `search`), and rates each model 1–3 in `tiers` against the others of its kind. `credits: null` means no single figure describes that price; it never means free. Binding a different model is `kipory-build`'s `references/models.md`.
- **The usual saving is a model step that should have been a decision.** A `text.generate` or `vocabulary.resolve` step answering a yes/no or a closed pick costs many times what `text.decide` charges for the same answer (`kipory-build`'s `references/models.md`, section 1).
- **Vendor prices are a read too.** `GET /v1/nodes/{nodeId}/vendor-prices` lists every paid fetch as a `provider` and an `operation`, with `credits` for one `unit` (`call` or `item`). A source function's page (`kipory-build`'s `references/functions/`) has a **Charged as** line naming the rows a step on it is charged under, per vendor where the step's `vendor.use` chooses one, and what one item is. `GET /v1/functions/{key}` carries the same facts as `charges` (and `providers.options[].charges` per vendor), for a script that joins them to the price rows. A function whose vendors that read lists under `servedBy` (`x.posts`) is charged the rows of the vendor that served the step's run, and the run's step detail names it in its own `servedBy`. Where the item is one of the vendor's own credits, the line and `charges.vendorCredits` say how many a request takes, which options raise it and how many requests a step makes: a read whose request takes several credits costs that many times the row's `credits`, and a paged list pays the per-request figure once per page. The vendor reports the real count on each response and the charge follows it. Read these before choosing a vendor, a `maxItems` or a polling period.
- **A `vendor-fetch` charge is `credits` × the units billed, per request or job at the vendor.** A read that pages makes one charge per page; a search the vendor runs as one job makes one. `includedUnits` are free on each before `credits` applies; `minimumUnits` are billed on each however few came back, an empty answer included. So a row with either is not a flat per-item price: a function charged a flat call plus the items beyond its included units costs the same for a handful of posts as for the whole allowance, and one charged per item with a floor pays the floor again for every page, the empty ones too. A step its `vendor.switch` moved to another vendor is charged that vendor's rows.
- **`credits: null` is never free by itself.** Beside `billing: "quota-free"` the operation is not charged. Beside `billing: "metered"` no price is set for it on this deployment, so none can be quoted; do not plan on it being free. `credits: 0` is a price of zero somebody set.
- **The list is the platform's price on the platform's key.** A vendor key the project stores moves that bill to the vendor and the step makes no `vendor-fetch` charge (`kipory-secrets`). A cached answer makes none either (below).
- **A keyed request makes no vendor charge.** A `url.fetch`, `url.fetch-as-file` or `url.send` step is charged its compute like any step; the service it calls bills the holder of the key the step names. The delivery of a `url.send` request after the run, and its retries, are not charged.

### When a price or a model changes under the project

A task binding above the project (the platform's or the organization's) can move the task to another model, and a price can change either way. Each change that reaches work the project paid for in the last seven days records one `platform/price.changed` event — with the old and new price of each kind of work and the day's credits before and after at last week's use — and raises a "Prices changed" alert on the project (`kipory-operate`'s `references/when-a-run-fails.md` lists the event types; the triggers pack has the event's fields). A trigger on it can mail you. To keep a model whatever the platform's binding does, bind the task on the project: `PUT /v1/nodes/{nodeId}/task-models/{task}`.

### A cache hit

A run that repeats an input can cost far less than the first. Two caches answer a step, they cost differently, and not every kind of run reads both:

| The step was answered from                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | It is charged                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| the step-result cache (same step, same settings, same inputs). Read by endpoint, schedule and trigger runs, and by a record's processing only when a `record.enqueue-process` step queued it with `mode: "from-cache"`. Not read by previews, eval runs, a record's first processing or `POST /v1/records/{id}/reprocess`. Only steps whose function keeps one are answered from it: `text.generate`, `audio.transcribe`, the file and image readers and `location.resolve`. A fetch or a `text.decide` step is answered only by the cache below | nothing — the step makes no charge at all                 |
| a function's input-keyed cache (a fetched page, a model answer to one prompt), kept for the time the function's page states. Read by every kind of run except an eval run of a suite with `subjectUncached: true` (the default), which looks nothing up and still stores its result                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | its compute fee for the lookup; no model or vendor charge |

Predict a range, and reconcile against `/spend` rather than a fixed number.

### `uncharged`

Each step, and the run, carries `uncharged`: how many of its `events` the platform paid for and charged to nobody.

- `credits: 0, events: 6, uncharged: 6` is work nobody was charged for. The same step in a charged run has a price, so do not budget from it.
- `credits: 0` with `uncharged: 0` is a real zero.
- A step that failed on a platform fault — `detail.phase: "platform-fault"` in the timeline, one of the platform's own vendor or model accounts refusing the call (the run's class is then `platform-account`) — is one of these: its compute is waived, so it reads `credits: 0` with its event counted in `uncharged`. Any other failed step is charged its compute as usual.
- `GET /v1/eval-runs/{id}/spend` carries the same field.

### A run the platform started again

An unattended run — a schedule's occurrence, a trigger's fire, an asynchronous endpoint call, a record's processing — whose attempt ended on one of the platform's own failures (`platform-busy`, `platform-interrupted`, `platform-fault`, or `platform-account` from a throttled platform account) is started again under the same run id, up to three attempts <!-- count: run-attempts --> in all (`references/when-a-run-fails.md`). The later attempt runs the steps again:

- **Compute is charged once per step per run**, however often the step is repeated.
- **A model or vendor call that is really made again is charged again**, as any call is. One a cache answers costs nothing more.
- **`repeatedByPlatform { credits, lines }` on `GET /v1/runs/{runId}/spend` is that share**: what the attempts after the first charged — calls made again, plus steps the first attempt never reached. It is included in `credits` and `byStep`, not added to them, and reads `{ credits: 0, lines: 0 }` on a run never started again.
- ⚠️ **The worst case is three attempts <!-- count: run-attempts -->**: a run whose worker is lost on every attempt but the last can pay for the same model or vendor call three times <!-- count: run-attempts -->. A schedule occurrence's `creditCost` includes it. Budget from `/spend`, not from one clean run.

## What each kind of run costs, and where to read it

| Work                                                  | Charged as                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Read it at                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| A synchronous product call                            | end-user work, to the wallet the caller draws on                                                                                                                                                                                                             | `x-credits-charged` on the response; `GET /v1/runs/{runId}/spend` by step                                    |
| An asynchronous product call                          | the same charge, made after the 202                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | `/spend` with the 202's `id`. The 202's own `x-credits-charged` was sent before the run and does not hold it |
| A schedule occurrence, a trigger's fire               | like any live run of the flow                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | `creditCost` on `GET /v1/schedules/{id}/runs`; `/spend` with the occurrence's `invocation.id`                |
| A record's first processing                           | charged, model calls, embeddings and vector writes included                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `/spend` on the processing run (`kipory-diagnose` finds its id)                                              |
| A reprocess (`POST /v1/records/{id}/reprocess`)       | like the first processing: the step-result cache is not read. A function's input-keyed cache still is, so a page fetched or a prompt answered inside its window is charged compute only. Every run it queues through `record.enqueue-process` is charged too | `/spend` on the run. It settles to the project's wallet unless the record's own owner asked for it           |
| A queued preview (`POST /v1/flows/{id}/preview-runs`) | design-time work, the same price as a live run, whether or not `apply` is `false`                                                                                                                                                                            | `/spend` with the `runId` the 202 answered                                                                   |
| An inline preview (`POST /v1/flows/{id}/preview`)     | the same design-time charge                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | it leaves no run to read: `GET /v1/projects/{nodeId}/usage/events?scope=design`                              |
| An eval run                                           | design-time work: every subject run, plus every scorer call                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `GET /v1/eval-runs/{id}/spend` by step, subject and scorers alike; `run.credits` on the run (`kipory-prove`) |
| Files and vectors the project keeps                   | a daily holding charge                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | `GET /v1/projects/{nodeId}/usage?scope=holding`                                                              |

Reprocessing 100 records after a flow edit costs what the edited flow costs on each — about what ingesting them did when the edit added no paid step, and less where earlier steps are answered from a function's cache. Read one run's `/spend` first.

To price a flow before it runs live, queue one preview with `apply: false` and read its `/spend`. A cold worker makes that first run slower, not dearer.
